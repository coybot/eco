# Quickstart Guide

This guide will help you get your Presidio drone in the air quickly.

## Prerequisites

- Presidio drone (Mothership or Scout)
- Charged battery (included)
- FlySky i6 controller (included)
- Presidio mobile app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/astral-us-drone-operator/id6471107516) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=us.astral.mobile))
- Open outdoor area (minimum 10m x 10m)

## Step 2: Charge & Install Battery

1. Charge the battery using the included charger (takes ~2 hours for full charge)
2. Slide the battery into the drone until it clicks
3. The drone LED will flash indicating power

## Step 3: Power On

1. **Controller first**: Turn on the FlySky i6 controller
2. **Drone second**: Press and hold the power button on the drone for 2 seconds
3. Wait for the LED to turn solid (indicates GPS lock)

<Warning>
Always power on the controller BEFORE the drone to ensure proper binding.
</Warning>

## Step 4: Connect App

1. Open the Presidio app
2. Tap "Connect Drone"
3. Select your drone from the list
4. The app will show "Connected" with live telemetry

## Step 5: First Flight

1. Place the drone on flat ground
2. Stand at least 3 meters away
3. In the app, tap "Arm"
4. Slowly push the throttle up to take off
5. Use the right stick to control direction

<Tip>
For your first flight, enable "Beginner Mode" in the app settings. This limits speed and altitude.
</Tip>

## Step 6: Landing

1. Slowly reduce throttle to descend
2. Land on a flat surface
3. Hold throttle down for 2 seconds to disarm
4. Or tap "Return to Home" for automatic landing

## Troubleshooting

### Drone won't connect

- Ensure controller is on first
- Check that drone battery is charged
- Move away from WiFi interference

### No GPS lock

- Move to an open outdoor area
- Wait up to 2 minutes for satellite acquisition
- Ensure clear view of sky (no buildings/trees directly above)

### App won't find drone

- Enable Bluetooth on your phone
- Ensure drone is powered on
- Try restarting both the app and drone
